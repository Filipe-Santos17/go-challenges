# The Temperatures of the Week

A weather station sends, every day at midnight, seven temperatures (one for each day of the previous week) in degrees Celsius. You receive these values and need to:

- Calculate the weekly average, the lowest and the highest temperature.
- Identify whether there was any day with a temperature below zero (frost).
- The temperatures must be stored in a fixed structure of seven positions.

Hint: Consider the most suitable numeric type and a data structure with fixed size. Use a constant for the number of days.
